# Research Method Patterns

## Packaging Purpose

This reference supports `research-method-curator/SKILL.md` by keeping reusable method patterns outside the main instruction file.

## Durable Research Package Rules

- Preserve raw AI outputs separately from synthesis.
- Maintain a source register before making decisions.
- Use a claim ledger for high-risk, clinical, legal, pricing, eligibility, model-selection, RAG, and CDSS claims.
- Label evidence strength explicitly.
- Separate research-only material from decision-ready material.
- Recheck live-changing facts at task time.
