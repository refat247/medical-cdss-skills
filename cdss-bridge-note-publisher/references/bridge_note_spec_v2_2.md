# Davidson Bridge Note Generation Specification (V2.2 Standard)

## Core Philosophy & The Dual-Truth Architecture
1. **Curricular Baseline Truth**: Davidson 25th Edition is the primary curriculum anchor. Its definitions, examination frameworks, and sequences are protected from external dilution in Layer 2.
2. **Acute Lethal Exception Layer**: Harrison 22nd Edition and Hurst 15th Edition serve as the high-yield acute resuscitation and exam-trap layer (quarantined in QB Awareness). Where chronic textbook rules fail in acute emergencies (e.g., acute severe MR producing an early decrescendo murmur or no murmur at all due to sudden LA pressure equalization), Harrison and Hurst provide the life-saving exception.
3. **Format Parity Protocol**: Zero raw ASCII art (`+---`, `|`) in either Markdown or Word. Markdown must use responsive card tables, and Word must use native XML card grid tables with executive styling.
4. **Biophysical Provenance Guard**: Layer 0 biophysics must describe fluid velocity, pressure gradients ($\Delta P$), and turbulence transition strictly qualitatively. Extrinsic mathematical formulas ($Re$, Torricelli equations) are strictly barred unless explicitly printed in the retrieved textbook chunk.
5. **Universal Chunk Anchoring**: Every vocabulary term, active recall flashcard, and quick revision bullet must cite its originating textbook chunk ID.

## The 6 Mandatory Structural Layers:

### 1. Coverage Declaration
Border-accented callout card explicitly stating:
- Primary scope & assigned chapter
- What this note covers (`✓`)
- What this note excludes (`✗`)
- Related notes & prerequisites (`→`)
- Dual-truth declaration (Davidson curricular core vs. Harrison/Hurst acute exception layer)

### 2. Visual Assets Packet
Structured table cataloging all figures across the 4 textbooks:
`| Asset ID | Title | Classification (CORE/SUPPORTIVE) | Information Exclusivity | Integration Mode | Coverage Method |`

### 3. Layer 0 — Physiology Foundation
Qualitative biophysical origin of sounds and murmurs:
- Silent laminar vs. turbulent flow transition
- Pressure gradient magnitude ($\Delta P$) and orifice velocity
- Wiggers diagram coupling (electrical-mechanical-acoustic phase alignment)

### 4. Layer 1 — Pre-Training Terms Table
5-Column medical vocabulary table with mandatory source attribution:
`| Term | Meaning | Why It Matters Clinically | Clinical Example | Textbook Provenance |`

### 5. Layer 2 — Protected Davidson Core Spine
**Inviolable primary truth.** Directly cites Davidson 25th Edition boxes, tables, and figures:
- 7-Attribute Auscultation Framework (presented as modern card grid, zero ASCII)
- Levine 6-point murmur grading scale and thrill threshold
- Core systolic and diastolic murmurs
- Benign vs. Pathological criteria
- Peripheral signs of aortic regurgitation (Box 16.89)
*Rule: Zero non-Davidson textbook content allowed into Layer 2.*

### 6. QB Awareness / Exam Trap Warning Box
Dedicated border-accented alert card (`#D93025` crimson left bar, warm `#FFF8E1` fill) detailing:
- Acute emergency lethal exceptions (from Harrison Ch 44)
- Maneuver paradoxes (Brockenbrough sign post-PVC in HOCM vs AS from Hurst Ch 2)
- Board examination negative traps

### 7. Layer 3 — Pharmacology Context
Autonomic and vascular mechanisms governing bedside maneuvers:
- Amyl nitrite (arteriolar vasodilation $\to$ drops SVR $\to$ increases ejection murmurs, softens regurgitant leaks)
- Phenylephrine / Handgrip (alpha-1 vasoconstriction $\to$ surges SVR $\to$ intensifies MR/AR/VSD, softens HOCM)

### 8. Layer 4 — Beyond Davidson Multi-Book
High-yield visual assets and differentials that Davidson mentions but does not illustrate:
- Harrison phonocardiograms and acoustic timing envelopes
- Hurst dynamic auscultation multi-maneuver matrix
- Kumar & Clark pediatric shunt dynamics (VSD, PDA continuous murmur, Eisenmenger syndrome)

### 9. Clinical Evidence Notes
Class of Recommendation (Class I, IIa, IIb, III) from ESC 2021 and AHA/ACC 2020 Valvular Heart Disease Guidelines for echocardiography and intervention triggers.

### 10. Layer 5 — Active Recall & Quick Revision
- **Active Recall Flashcards**: 10 high-yield clinical question-prompts, each ending with an explicit source chunk citation (`[Anchor: Book-Ch-ChunkID]`).
- **Quick Revision Box**: 10 high-yield rapid review bullets with chunk traceability.
