# Angina and trimetazidine — doctor defense

Citation map. Books, then guidelines, then what not to say.

Ward limit: Not essential. Not a survival drug. Class IIb in ESC 2024 CCS. Absent from AHA 2023 CCD. Do not use in Parkinson disease, pregnancy, breastfeeding, or creatinine clearance below 30 mL/min.

## Books
- Slide 2: 40% said angina affected quality of life completely or very much. BRIDGE 2. Say: Leftover angina is a real burden. The 40% is that survey. Locator: Slide 2 only.
- Slide 3: Angina at 1 year linked to 32% higher cardiovascular death or infarction. CLARIFY. Say: Persistent symptoms mark higher risk. This figure does not prove the drug prevents those deaths. Locator: Slide 3. Direction without 32%: Harrison L2-622, Davidson L2-164.

## Guidelines
- Slide 8: CCS is a transient demand-supply mismatch from arteries and/or microcirculation. Say: Use the 2024 definition if challenged. Locator: ESC 2024 CCS PDF p9-10. Davidson L2-155. Harrison L2-610. Kumar L2-280.
- Slide 9: About 50% have non-obstructive disease. Say: Davidson says 10-20% of effort angina have normal coronaries. Selected no-obstruction meta-analysis: about 41% microvascular and 40% vasospastic. Locator: Davidson L2-156. ESC 2024 CCS PDF p31.
- Slide 43: Class moved from IIa to IIb. Say: Nicorandil or trimetazidine may be considered, Class IIb. Nitrates and ranolazine stayed IIa. Locator: ESC 2024 CCS PDF p16 Table 4. Gap: PDF p84.
- Slide 44: AHA agrees. Say: AHA 2023 does not list trimetazidine and uses ranolazine. Ivabradine is Class 3 Harm if the ventricle is normal. Locator: AHA 2023 CCD PDF p51.

## Do not say
- Slide 1: Trimetazidine is an essential part of therapy. Say: It may be considered as add-on, or in a selected first combination, Class IIb. Locator: ESC 2024 CCS PDF p16 Table 4. Harrison L2-634 says second-line, not essential.
- Slide 12: Nitrates worsen endothelium in spasm. Say: Nitrates act on large vessels and preload. They relieve spasm acutely. Isolated spasm: calcium-channel blocker, Class I, Level A. Locator: Harrison L2-658. ESC 2024 CCS PDF p15.
- Slide 18: Trimetazidine gives +33% ATP. Say: Harrison states the shift from fatty-acid oxidation toward glucose. The percentage is Filmore 2014 on the slide. Locator: Harrison L2-634. Slides 15-18 for +33%.
