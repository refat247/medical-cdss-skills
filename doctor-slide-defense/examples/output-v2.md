# Angina and trimetazidine — doctor defense

Angina is a mismatch, not only a tight artery. This drug changes fuel use when you cannot lower demand further. Europe allows that at Class IIb.

Ward limit: Not essential. Not a survival drug. Class IIb in ESC 2024 CCS. Absent from AHA 2023 CCD. Do not use in Parkinson disease, pregnancy, breastfeeding, or creatinine clearance below 30 mL/min.

## Slides 1 to 5 — leftover angina

The early slides are about the patient, not the molecule.
It may be considered as add-on, or in a selected first combination, Class IIb. (ESC 2024 CCS PDF p16 Table 4. Harrison L2-634 says second-line, not essential.) Leftover angina is a real burden. The 40% is that survey. (Slide 2 only.) Persistent symptoms mark higher risk. This figure does not prove the drug prevents those deaths. (Slide 3. Direction without 32%: Harrison L2-622, Davidson L2-164.)

## Slides 6 to 12 — not one blocked artery

Ischaemia is a mismatch. A tight stenosis is the usual cause, not the only one.
Use the 2024 definition if challenged. (ESC 2024 CCS PDF p9-10. Davidson L2-155. Harrison L2-610. Kumar L2-280.) Davidson says 10-20% of effort angina have normal coronaries. Selected no-obstruction meta-analysis: about 41% microvascular and 40% vasospastic. (Davidson L2-156. ESC 2024 CCS PDF p31.) Nitrates act on large vessels and preload. They relieve spasm acutely. Isolated spasm: calcium-channel blocker, Class I, Level A. (Harrison L2-658. ESC 2024 CCS PDF p15.)

## Slides 15 to 18 — metabolism

Defend the direction of the fuel shift. Do not defend the percentage.
Harrison states the shift from fatty-acid oxidation toward glucose. The percentage is Filmore 2014 on the slide. (Harrison L2-634. Slides 15-18 for +33%.)

## Slides 43 and 44 — class change

The downgrade is printed. The congress anecdote is not.
Nicorandil or trimetazidine may be considered, Class IIb. Nitrates and ranolazine stayed IIa. (ESC 2024 CCS PDF p16 Table 4. Gap: PDF p84.) AHA 2023 does not list trimetazidine and uses ranolazine. Ivabradine is Class 3 Harm if the ventricle is normal. (AHA 2023 CCD PDF p51.)
