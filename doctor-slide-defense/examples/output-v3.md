# Angina and trimetazidine — doctor defense

## How to defend the talk
### The line you must not cross

Angina is a mismatch, not only a tight artery. This drug changes fuel use when you cannot lower demand further. Europe allows that at Class IIb.

Not essential. Not a survival drug. Class IIb in ESC 2024 CCS. Absent from AHA 2023 CCD. Do not use in Parkinson disease, pregnancy, breastfeeding, or creatinine clearance below 30 mL/min.

## The talk

### Slides 1 to 5 — leftover angina

The early slides are about the patient, not the molecule.

The slide says: Trimetazidine is an essential part of therapy.
What you may say: It may be considered as add-on, or in a selected first combination, Class IIb.
If asked: Essential is the slide title, not the guideline.
Source: ESC 2024 CCS PDF p16 Table 4. Harrison L2-634 says second-line, not essential.

The slide says: 40% said angina affected quality of life completely or very much. BRIDGE 2.
What you may say: Leftover angina is a real burden. The 40% is that survey.
If asked: Quality of life is why symptom drugs exist. The percentage is not a textbook fact.
Source: Slide 2 only.

The slide says: Angina at 1 year linked to 32% higher cardiovascular death or infarction. CLARIFY.
What you may say: Persistent symptoms mark higher risk. This figure does not prove the drug prevents those deaths.
If asked: CLARIFY is observational.
Source: Slide 3. Direction without 32%: Harrison L2-622, Davidson L2-164.


### Slides 6 to 12 — not one blocked artery

Ischaemia is a mismatch. A tight stenosis is the usual cause, not the only one.

The slide says: CCS is a transient demand-supply mismatch from arteries and/or microcirculation.
What you may say: Use the 2024 definition if challenged.
If asked: Disease is the pathology. Syndrome is the presentation.
Source: ESC 2024 CCS PDF p9-10. Davidson L2-155. Harrison L2-610. Kumar L2-280.

The slide says: About 50% have non-obstructive disease.
What you may say: Davidson says 10-20% of effort angina have normal coronaries. Selected no-obstruction meta-analysis: about 41% microvascular and 40% vasospastic.
If asked: The slide graphic and the retrieved sentences are different facts.
Source: Davidson L2-156. ESC 2024 CCS PDF p31.

The slide says: Nitrates worsen endothelium in spasm.
What you may say: Nitrates act on large vessels and preload. They relieve spasm acutely. Isolated spasm: calcium-channel blocker, Class I, Level A.
If asked: The wrong first drug in isolated spasm is the beta-blocker.
Source: Harrison L2-658. ESC 2024 CCS PDF p15.


### Slides 15 to 18 — metabolism

Defend the direction of the fuel shift. Do not defend the percentage.

The slide says: Trimetazidine gives +33% ATP.
What you may say: Harrison states the shift from fatty-acid oxidation toward glucose. The percentage is Filmore 2014 on the slide.
If asked: How much more ATP? The textbook does not give a percentage.
Source: Harrison L2-634. Slides 15-18 for +33%.


### Slides 43 and 44 — class change

The downgrade is printed. The congress anecdote is not.

The slide says: Class moved from IIa to IIb.
What you may say: Nicorandil or trimetazidine may be considered, Class IIb. Nitrates and ranolazine stayed IIa.
If asked: Do not say the PDF declares the downgrade illegitimate.
Source: ESC 2024 CCS PDF p16 Table 4. Gap: PDF p84.

The slide says: AHA agrees.
What you may say: AHA 2023 does not list trimetazidine and uses ranolazine. Ivabradine is Class 3 Harm if the ventricle is normal.
If asked: Say AHA does not endorse this deck.
Source: AHA 2023 CCD PDF p51.
