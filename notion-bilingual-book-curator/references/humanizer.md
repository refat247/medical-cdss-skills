# Humanizer-Style Editorial Standard

Apply only after source/evidence reconciliation.

## May Improve

- robotic phrasing;
- repetitive conclusions;
- canned transitions;
- bloated sentences;
- paragraph rhythm;
- excessive headings/bolding;
- unnatural Bengali.

## Must Preserve

- every fact;
- every number/date;
- citations and URLs;
- evidence labels;
- uncertainty;
- warnings;
- project decisions;
- clinical/regulatory meaning;
- technical literals.

## Do Not Freely Rewrite

- quotations;
- source excerpts;
- code/logs;
- exact benchmark fields;
- qrel/chunk IDs;
- raw evidence tables;
- immutable source text.

## Semantic-Drift Check

After editing verify:
- no new claims;
- no dropped claims;
- no stronger claims;
- no softened warnings;
- no changed numbers/dates;
- no changed uncertainty;
- no changed evidence state;
- no changed project decision.
