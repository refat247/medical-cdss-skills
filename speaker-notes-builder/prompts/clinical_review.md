# Clinical/CME review prompt contract

Audit every substantive script claim against the deck and approved sources.

High-risk categories include dose, titration, indication, local regulatory approval, contraindication, boxed warning, pregnancy/lactation, paediatric use, interactions, comparative efficacy, mortality/MACE, procedure/anaesthesia advice, and off-label use.

For each high-risk claim:
1. identify exact support;
2. classify status;
3. preserve jurisdiction/date qualifiers;
4. block or rewrite unsupported claims;
5. do not strengthen trial signals into indications;
6. do not convert foreign-label authorization into Bangladesh/local authorization without the local label.
