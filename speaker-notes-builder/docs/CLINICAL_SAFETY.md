# Clinical/CME safety model

The skill is an authoring and validation workflow, not a medical authority.

## Claim status
- `SUPPORTED`: directly supported by approved source material.
- `EXPLANATORY_ONLY`: adds explanation/transition but no new factual medical claim.
- `SOURCE_PARTIAL`: source supports part but not all of the wording.
- `SOURCE_CONFLICT`: approved sources conflict.
- `JURISDICTION_SENSITIVE`: correctness depends on country/agency/product label/date.
- `UNVERIFIED`: not verified from allowed sources.
- `BLOCKED`: must not enter final spoken text until repaired.

## Jurisdiction rule

A statement such as “approved for X” must carry the jurisdiction/product context that supports it. A US FDA label does not prove Bangladesh/DGDA authorization.

## Dose rule

Dose, titration, missed-dose instructions, age cut-offs, pregnancy advice, and contraindications are high-risk. They require explicit source support or must be qualified/blocked.

## Trial-vs-indication rule

Promising trial evidence, subgroup signals, mechanistic plausibility, or publication of a positive study does not itself create a licensed indication.
