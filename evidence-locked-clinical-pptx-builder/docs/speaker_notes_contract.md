# Speaker Notes Evidence-Lock Contract

Notes may preserve detail omitted for projector readability, but all clinical content remains source-locked. Notes should carry exact provenance, complete qualifiers, Class/LoE, dose/threshold/timing, uncertainty, terminology differences, operational-vs-guideline distinction, reveal instruction and what not to overclaim.

A notes-presence check is insufficient for semantic certification; final release requires notes content/provenance audit as well as XML presence.
